This policy explains what the Elric AI app (the Android app, the iPhone/PC web app at elricai.sbs, and related versions) does with your information. Elric AI is made by Brahma Reddy Suravarapu, Elric AI, Narasaraopet, Andhra Pradesh, India ("we", "us").

The short version

  • Online answers are optional, for users 18 or older only, and off by default (Android app and web app). You turn them on in ☰ → Online answers, after confirming you are 18 or older, and can turn them off anytime. When they are on and you are online, your question, recent messages of the chat, any photo you attach and the Memory used for that answer are sent to our server and an AI provider to answer. We do not store them.
  • Offline answers are processed on your own device and are not sent anywhere. With Online answers off (the default), all answers are offline.
  • No ads. We do not sell your data. No account is needed.
  • "About you" and Memory stay on your device, and you can delete them anytime.
  • When you tap Report on an answer, that question and answer are sent to Elric for review. Feedback and error reports are optional and off by default. If you send them, they are stored on Supabase, whose servers may be outside India.
  • When online, the app downloads a short list of facts to answer more reliably. Nothing about you is sent.

1. Chats and the AI model

Online answers (Android app and web app). The setting "Online answers" is optional, off by default, and only for users 18 or older: it turns on only after you confirm in the app that you are 18 or older, and you can turn it off anytime in ☰ → Online answers. If you are under 18, please leave it off. When it is on and your device is online, each question is answered through our server: the question, up to the last 12 messages of that chat (so Elric understands follow-ups), any photo you attach (reduced in size), your name (and class, if set) from "About you", the Memory notes used for that answer, and your chosen mode, language and answer length are sent to our server (a Supabase Edge Function) and from there to our AI provider, Google (the Gemini API), which generates the answer. If online answers are slow, unavailable or the connection drops, the app answers on your device instead (in the web app, if the offline model is not loaded, it asks you to load it); each answer is marked "Online" or "Offline". To limit abuse, the app sends a random app ID created on your device (in the web app it is kept in your browser's storage).

  • What we keep. We do not store or log your questions, answers, photos or Memory on our server. To prevent abuse we keep only a counter of questions per hour for a random app ID and for your IP address, both stored as one-way hashes (not the ID or IP itself), deleted after about two days.
  • What the provider may keep. Google processes the request under its own terms. We currently use its free service tier, under which Google may store requests and answers and use them to improve its products, and trained reviewers may read them. Please do not share sensitive personal information (such as ID numbers, passwords, health or financial details) while Online answers are on, or turn the setting off.
  • Your saved chats stay on your phone either way.

Offline answers. When Online answers are off (the default) or you are offline, the answer is generated by an AI model running on your phone or computer. Your messages and the answers are not sent anywhere.

To make this work, the AI model is downloaded once from the internet, from the host where the model file is published. That download works like downloading any file: the host sees a normal web request (such as your IP address and device/browser type), but not your chats. After the download, the model runs on your device and Elric works without internet for these features.

The web app also loads its own app files from elricai.sbs, which is served through a standard web hosting provider. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address and time of request). We do not use these to identify you.

2. "About you" and Memory

If you tell Elric your name, class or other details in "About you", or if Elric saves notes in Memory, this information is stored only on your device. It is used only to make answers more useful to you. With Online answers on, your name, class and the Memory notes used for a question are sent along with that question (see section 1) and are not stored by us.

You can view and delete it at any time from the menu (☰ → Memory, or ☰ → About you). Uninstalling the app (or clearing the web app's site data in your browser) deletes it as well. Saved chats are also stored only on your device.

3. Voice input

In the Android app, voice (the microphone button and Talk mode) is turned into text on your phone by the Elric model; the recording is not uploaded and is not kept. In Talk mode, answers are read aloud with your phone's built-in text-to-speech. The text of your question is then answered like any typed question (online or offline, see section 1). You can always type instead.

In the web app, voice input (the microphone button) and Talk use your browser's built-in speech recognition. Elric does not record or upload your voice itself, but your browser or operating system may send the audio to its own speech service to turn it into text (for example Apple on iPhone and iPad, or your browser's maker on other devices), under that provider's privacy policy; on iPhone this may need an internet connection. Answers in Talk, and when you tap 🔊, are read aloud by your device's built-in text-to-speech. If you prefer, just type.

4. Camera and photos

In the Android app, if you use the camera or pick a photo, it is saved in the app on your device. With Online answers on, the photo (reduced in size) is sent with your question to our server and the AI provider to answer, and is not stored by us (see section 1). Offline, the photo is processed on your device and is not sent anywhere.

In the web app, photos work only with Online answers on: the photo you take or pick is reduced in size in your browser and sent with your question to our server and the AI provider to answer, and is not stored by us. Saved chats in your browser keep only a "📷 Photo" note, not the photo.

5. Feedback, error reports and the facts list

Feedback and error reports are available in the Android app; the web app has "Report wrong answer". They are opt-in: nothing is sent unless you choose to.

  • Feedback. When you tap 👎 on an answer, you can choose "Wrong answer" or "Not helpful" and add an optional note. It is sent only if you tap Send. The question and answer are included only if you tick "Include this question and answer" (off by default). Feedback also includes the app version, phone model, Android version, which Elric model and backend (CPU/GPU) were used, and the mode.
  • Report wrong answer (web app). When you tap Report (👎 under an answer) and then Send, that question and answer are sent to Elric for review, with your optional note, the app version, browser and device type, whether the answer was online or offline, the Elric model and the mode. No other chats are sent.
  • Error reports. Off by default; you can turn them on or off anytime in ☰ → Privacy ("Send error reports"), and the app asks you once. When on, if the app crashes, a report is saved on your phone and sent the next time you open the app; if the model gets stuck or fails while loading, a short report is sent. Error reports contain the app version, phone model, Android version, Elric model, backend and technical error details (error type and code location). They never contain your chats.
  • Where it is stored. Feedback and error reports are stored in a database run by Supabase, our backend provider. Its servers may be outside India. Reports are used only to fix problems and improve answers, and are never sold or used for ads. To ask for deletion, contact us at the email below.
  • Facts list. When your device is online, the app downloads a short list of facts from our server (at most once a day) and keeps it on the device, so answers stay accurate even offline. This download sends no information about you or your chats; like any web request, the server sees basic technical details such as your IP address.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking in the app.

7. Children

Elric AI is made for students. Children under 13 should use it with a parent or guardian. Offline answers (the default) work for everyone. Online answers are only for users 18 or older, because our AI provider's terms require it; they are off by default and turn on only after the user confirms they are 18 or older. No account is needed and we do not store questions sent for online answers, so we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents can keep Online answers off in ☰ so all answers stay on the device.

8. AI answers can be wrong

Elric tries to say when it is not sure, but AI answers can still be wrong or out of date. Please check important facts (for exams, health, money, legal or safety matters) with a teacher, a trusted source or a professional.

10. Your choices and rights

Your chats, "About you" and Memory are kept on your device, so you are in control: you can turn Online answers off anytime in ☰ → Online answers, you can delete "About you", Memory and chats in the app, or remove everything by uninstalling. You can turn error reports off anytime in ☰ → Privacy, and ask us to delete feedback or reports you sent. If you have a question or request about your information, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, contact us at the email below.
