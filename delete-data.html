Elric AI has no user accounts. Your chats, name and settings are stored only on your phone.

Delete data on your phone

  • Delete a chat: open the menu (☰), find it under Recents and tap ×.
  • Delete the offline model: Profile → Offline mode → Delete model.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's Settings → Apps → Elric AI → Storage → Clear data.

Request deletion of data on our server

Online questions are used only to create the answer and are not stored. A hashed device ID used for rate limits is deleted automatically after 2 days. Wrong-answer and error reports you choose to send are kept to improve answers.

To ask us to delete reports linked to your device, email theinfo222@gmail.com with the subject "Elric AI data deletion" and your phone model and app version (Profile → About). We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.
